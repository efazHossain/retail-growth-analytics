# Project Notes: Retail Growth Analytics

## Business Problem

The company wants to grow revenue without sacrificing margin. Leadership needs a practical view of which categories, channels, and regions are driving performance, plus a clear recommendation on where to focus next.

## Current Approach

This project uses an end-to-end analytics workflow that turns transaction-level order data into repeatable summaries and visual checks. It includes raw CSV data, a transformation script, SQL business questions, KPI summaries, and a local dashboard.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Data modeling with customer and order tables
- KPI design for revenue, profit, margin, average order value, and fulfillment speed
- Segmentation by category, channel, region, and month
- SQL query framing for common business questions
- Dashboard storytelling and executive summary writing

## Key Findings

- Revenue is growing month over month, but margin varies by region and channel.
- Product category mix is a major driver of revenue concentration.
- Discounting and fulfillment speed should be monitored because they can quietly reduce profitability.

## Recommendation

Prioritize growth campaigns where category demand and margin are both strong, then investigate discount leakage in lower-margin region and channel combinations before scaling spend.
